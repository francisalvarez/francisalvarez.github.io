A collection of video projects, visual storytelling work, and creative media. Below are examples of videos I’ve produced professionally, along with hobby projects created for my own personal archive.

## Independent Options

In November 2021, I was contracted by [Independent Options](https://iofamilyhomeagency.wixsite.com/mysite-1/blank/), an organization that coordinates independent and supported living for people with developmental disabilities, to produce two videos. The videos explained what a family housing agency is and highlighted examples of family home providers.

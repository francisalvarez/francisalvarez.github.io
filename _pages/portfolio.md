## Electric Vehicle Incentive Program Evaluation

### Overview
Evaluated whether a statewide electric vehicle incentive program effectively increased electric vehicle adoption while supporting equitable access to clean transportation.

### Methods
- Logistic regression
- Survey analysis
- Program evaluation
- Equity assessment
- Data visualization

### Key Findings
Developed a logistic regression model to identify participant characteristics associated with incentive reliance and potential free ridership. The analysis helped program administrators better understand which participants were most likely to need the incentive to complete their electric vehicle purchase.

### Impact
The results supported conversations about program equity, incentive design, and whether public funds were reaching participants most influenced by the rebate.

I’m Francis Alvarez, a data analyst with experience in program evaluation consulting.

## Career Objectives

I help organizations use data to make clear evidence-based decisions.

My professional background is in sustainability consulting, program evaluation, and applied research. Much of my work focuses on translating complex datasets into actionable insights for stakeholders, including program administrators, policymakers, and business decision-makers.

I am especially interested in roles where I can apply analytical methods to a focused product, program, or organization over time. My goal is to combine technical analysis, research design, and communication to help teams better understand users, measure outcomes, and improve decision-making.

## Personal Interests

### Videography

Ever since I was a teenager, I’ve enjoyed recording moments from my life, whether it was hanging out with friends, traveling to new places, or documenting everyday experiences. Growing up, I was fortunate to have some kind of device that allowed me to capture those moments. I started with a Nikon D3300, then caught the action-camera bug and experimented with several GoPro models. Currently, I’m enjoying my Fujifilm X-T4, which has been my daily driver for the past few years.

Using different cameras over the years naturally led me to vlog life experiences for my personal archive. It has also created a few opportunities to produce short-form content professionally for businesses.

There are a few things that drew me to videography. First, I’ve always liked having documented experiences that I can revisit later in life. Second, I enjoy the challenge of learning video editing software, starting with iMovie, then Final Cut Pro X, and now DaVinci Resolve. Learning these tools has also given me a creative outlet outside of analytics. Lastly, I find video editing relaxing. There is something therapeutic about crafting a visual story from footage I recorded and pairing it with whatever music I was enjoying at the time.

## Electric Vehicle Incentive Program Evaluation

### Objective

Evaluate whether a statewide electric vehicle incentive program effectively increased EV adoption while supporting equitable access to clean transportation.

### Methods

* Logistic regression modeling
* Survey analysis
* Program evaluation
* Equity assessment
* Statistical significance testing

### Key Findings

Developed a logistic regression model to identify characteristics associated with participants who would have purchased an electric vehicle without receiving an incentive ("free riders"). Results provided program administrators with a data-driven understanding of participant reliance on incentives and highlighted demographic differences in program impact.

### Impact

The analysis informed discussions around program equity and helped stakeholders evaluate whether public funds were reaching participants most influenced by the incentive.

**Tools:** R, Python, Excel, Survey Data Analysis
